# Gabled cedar porch roof proposal

The open porch retains its eight-foot clear square and six-inch step down. Its ridge projects outward from the existing doorway, with a 4:12 cross pitch. Cedar color, six-inch visible courses, and stagger match the main roof's appearance. The old low shed roof is retired.

The finished cedar ridge is at model Y=2.725 m. A 4 mm appearance layer sits on a 20 mm proposed deck; timber framing meets the deck underside. New roof framing starts outside the existing roof overhang. Overlapping deck and cedar volumes are trimmed above the actual main cedar surface. A stepped flashing strip closes the outer eave transition, while paired planar strips follow the central roof intersection. The original building timbers remain unchanged by this roof module.

The [Cedar Shake and Shingle Bureau roofing manual](https://www.cedarbureau.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/roof-manual-2020.pdf) recommends a minimum 4:12 pitch for shakes. Visible courses are an appearance choice, not a complete shake length, concealed lap, fastening, underlayment, or roof specification.

These are proposed occupied volumes and explicit junction surfaces. They do not establish structural capacity, ventilation, waterproofness, wind resistance, code compliance, or a construction-ready flashing detail. Roofing build-up, junction design, anchors and all loads require project-specific review. The main roof's own covering remains an appearance model.

The pure roof module exposes `OctagonMudroomRoof.spec(model)` and `build(model)`. Deck, cedar, and flashing have separate selectable meshes; all follow the porch layer and roof inspection control. Tests check positive closed surfaces, exact pitch, headroom, matching course settings, main-roof clearance and pairwise non-overlap in every model variant.
