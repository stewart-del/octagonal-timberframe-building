# Historical projections and R01 replacement

The NAV-05 baseline (`a431e6257848aafcb7b237eedbaece2ca44f3436`) partitioned overlapping stock and transferred assumed 40% keys. Its wings, narrow webs, cavities and overlap reports were properties of that generated prototype, not authenticated architect's joinery.

R01 replaces those carved solids with explicit member envelopes and source-based contact planes. None of the assumed 40% key sizes or historical zero-overlap claims is a current joint specification. Short rafters terminate at adjacent hip side faces. Ground-to-tie elevation, bearing offsets and deliberate mortise/tenon/fastener details remain unresolved.

Retained timber IDs preserve identity and A/B axis direction. They do not imply that the old cut vertices or finished dimensions remain unchanged. See `r01-change-register.json`, `JOINT-DESIGN-BRIEF.md` and the reproducible generator `scripts/revise_r01.py`.
