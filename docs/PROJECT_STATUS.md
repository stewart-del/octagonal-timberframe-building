# Octagonal timberframe prototype

NAV-04 preserves 104 individually identified timber members and 208 A/B end references from the recovered model. It adds keyboard and focus fixes, cut-centred inspection, a representative P0 corner view, and a practical connection-family review.

This is a geometric prototype for review. The current hip/ring bearing conflict remains unresolved. No structural capacity, machining allowance, practical raising sequence, or construction approval is established. Numerical fit results recorded in the earlier model are historical audit metadata.

Proposed connection families: supported ring-beam laps, housed tie tenons, housed brace tenons, hip bearing seats, secondary-rafter seats, and accessible cupola closure. Fabrication geometry and dimensions remain unresolved pending confirmed design inputs.
