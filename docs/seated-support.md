# Temporary metal support

The original simple eight radial L straps have been restored, including their original 1/8-inch display thickness and 24 bores. The later tangential arm offsets and five-inch central extensions have been removed. The 90-inch diametric span, four-inch strap depth and 14-inch total leg height are retained.

Placement retains the newer center and elevation, Y95–109 inches. A rigid −4.7° rotation clears the cupola posts without changing the form. Every hole axis reaches its corresponding post, with approximately 0.463 inch of air before entering about 6.02 inches of wood. This is a visible clearance, not flush seating or a designed fastener connection.

Only M-TMP is replaced in each model. Timber records remain identical. Current collision and movement checks must be recomputed after the change; updating source hashes alone does not constitute verification. Steel gauge, holes, fastening, welds and erection capacity remain display assumptions.
