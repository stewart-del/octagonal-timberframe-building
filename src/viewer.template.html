How to read the angles

Axis inclination describes the modeled member's direction. It is not an end-cut angle.

R03 end-plane reference records the original source-model end plane. These values do not describe every new surface in the fitted solid. Select an actual cut patch to inspect its plane angle and vertices.

22.5° conditional plan miter, where listed, assumes equal miters in a regular octagonal ring. The source joint still needs verification.

Mating surfaces shown here are a geometric fit prototype. They do not establish structural capacity, machining allowance or a construction release. R03 A/B planes are retained as earlier references; mating cut IDs describe the fitted surfaces.
