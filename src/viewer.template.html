How to read the angles

Axis inclination describes the member’s direction. Cut-plane angles are measured from the member axis; they are not saw settings.

Inside edge angles pass through timber material; over 180° identifies a concave corner. The acute plane angle is the smaller angle between the adjacent planes.

R03 A/B references are earlier end planes. The conditional 22.5° miter assumes an equal regular-octagon ring. Neither establishes a construction joint.
