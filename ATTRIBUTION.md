# Architectural reference and model provenance

The architectural reference is Robert Swinburne / Bluetime Collaborative’s [Octagon timber-frame outbuilding](https://www.bluetimecollaborative.com/robert-swinburne-vermont-architect/octagon-timber-frame-outbuilding).

This project is a reconstructed and modified geometric study. It is not the architect’s original CAD, not a reproduction of verified shop joinery, and not an endorsement by the architect. The displayed dimensions combine interpreted source callouts and explicitly provisional modeling choices. The eaves are modeled 2 ft 6 in beyond the assumed outer post envelope; finished cladding is not specified.

The fitted cuts are conceptual geometry. Repeated keys were generated from a 40% width/depth envelope and clipped against other stock. Structural sizing, machining allowances and an erection sequence were not established by that operation.

No source photography or original architectural sheet is redistributed in this Site. The review links primary references from the Timber Frame Engineering Council, USDA Forest Products Laboratory, American Wood Council and ASCE. Those references do not approve this model.

No blanket license for the referenced architectural work is asserted.
