R01 model downloads

These exports are generated from the same corrected geometry shown in the explorer. Retained timber IDs and their A/B axis references stay attached to each member.

Assembled model · GLBPermanent timber and spacer geometry, in meters with Y up.Exploded model · GLBSeparated parts for inspection; not an erection sequence.Complete model · JSONAll layers, geometry, contacts, dimensions and provenance.Revision register · JSONRetained and retired pieces, assumptions and geometry basis.

The GLB exports omit temporary erection equipment and the optional roof surface. Both remain available in the viewer and complete JSON model.

Contact faces describe geometric intersections. Mortises, tenons, fasteners, bearing offsets and final cutting allowances are not specified by these files.
