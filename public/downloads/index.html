The GLBs contain the canonical frame and cupola subset. The website also shows the porch, wall infill, floor, foundation and roof coverings, and checks their presence during disassembly. Temporary construction aids are excluded from the verified sequence.

The sequence checks geometric clearance, including already removed pieces. It does not establish structural capacity, safe temporary support or a construction procedure. Physical tolerances, moisture movement, design loads and connection capacities require further design.
