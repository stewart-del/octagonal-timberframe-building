Octagonal Timberframe Building

This interactive study lets you examine an octagonal timber-frame prototype as 104 individual solids. Select a member, isolate its fitted center, inspect either end, or navigate freely around its cut surfaces.

Use Free / WASD for movement; Q/E moves down/up, moving the mouse steers without dragging, and Shift moves faster. Click Free / WASD to capture the mouse, then press Escape to release it for selecting pieces and controls. The parts and materials dialogs also release capture. Dragging remains available if mouse capture is unsupported. Inspect End A/B chooses a fine movement speed. Keys pause while typing. No account or network service is required by the viewer itself.

Shift-click multiple timbers to keep them solid while the surrounding frame becomes transparent. Piece labels and small edge measurements start off; dark member edges and high contrast surfaces start on. The selected-piece panel lists recorded and measured model angles, plus a full edge table and CSV export. Edge measurements distinguish the inside material angle from the acute angle between adjoining face planes; neither is a saw setting. The materials list groups all 104 unchanged timber IDs into 13 numbered fitted-shape types, with eight pieces per type and a CSV export.

Hollows and recesses colors removed original stock amber, including space occupied by mating timber. The magenta Unfilled in assembly mode shows the unoccupied subset within the original stock. These colors represent space, not extra timber. Exterior trimming is not an internal defect, and the overlay is not a complete void or contact assessment.

Design status

The carved joints are conceptual. Review found severely narrowed sections, thin local walls and blocked tested insertion paths. They require redesign and engineering before construction. The fresh assembled fitted-solid check found zero member-pair overlaps above 1e-10 m³; that result does not approve bearing, retained wood, cuts, machining clearances or insertion paths. The assumed 40% key envelopes and hip/ring bearing-datum conflict remain unresolved.

Architectural reference

The starting reference is Robert Swinburne / Bluetime Collaborative’s octagon outbuilding. This is a reconstructed and modified study, not the architect’s original CAD or an endorsed construction drawing set. Several dimensions, member sections and all fitted joint details remain provisional. The recovered original 864 × 631 image has now been inspected: it is a building overview, not a dimensioned corner-joint detail. It does not authenticate the generated wing-like projections or concealed joinery.

The eaves are modeled 2 ft 6 in beyond the assumed outer post envelope. Finished cladding is unspecified. The temporary black center support is not part of the permanent 104-piece model.
