Octagonal Timberframe Building

This interactive study lets you examine an octagonal timber-frame prototype as 104 individual solids. Select a member, isolate its fitted center, inspect either end, or navigate freely around its cut surfaces.

Use Free / WASD for movement; Q/E moves down/up, drag looks, and Shift moves faster. Inspect End A/B chooses a fine movement speed. Keys pause while typing. No account or network service is required by the viewer itself.

Design status

The carved joints are conceptual. Review found severely narrowed sections, thin local walls and blocked tested insertion paths. They require redesign and engineering before construction.

Architectural reference

The starting reference is Robert Swinburne / Bluetime Collaborative’s octagon outbuilding. This is a reconstructed and modified study, not the architect’s original CAD or an endorsed construction drawing set. Several dimensions, member sections and all fitted joint details remain provisional.

The eaves are modeled 2 ft 6 in beyond the assumed outer post envelope. Finished cladding is unspecified. The temporary black center support is not part of the permanent 104-piece model.
