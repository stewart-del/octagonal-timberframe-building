R02: fitted cupola and restored inspection

Cupola posts extend down to the bottom of the 6 × 10 ties. Fitted octagonal 4 × 6 bands connect the posts. The main hips have flat rectangular tops, with no backing bevel. The P0 corner close-up, exploded assembly, end views, edge tables, multi-selection and free navigation are retained. Selecting wood automatically shows bright transparent recesses.

The outside and inside diameters imply a 6 in radial post depth and 41 in center radius. Rotated 6 × 6 posts give a 28.132865 in outward-corner gap, which rounds to 2 ft 4⅛ in. This gap is neither the post-center chord (31.380041 in) nor the clear span on the inside edge.

Readable source values and prior user directions govern the dimensions above. The source lower-member callout is 6 × 8; the model retains Stewart’s earlier explicit change to 6 × 10. The readable 4 × 6 bands are preserved. Glazing height 2 ft 8 in has uncertain endpoints. Roof timber depth, pegs, steel thickness and window dimensions cannot be established from this image.

The proposed fit uses centered shoulder tenons and matching sockets, diagonal brace sockets, and shallow bearing seats. Joint sizes are design assumptions, not source dimensions. The main roof height is offset by the actual member depth to bear above the ties; the ring is aligned with the short-rafter underside. These inferred bearing datums preserve plan dimensions and slopes. Structural capacity, timber movement and assembly sequence remain unverified.

The new rafter-to-hip connection

Each short rafter runs perpendicular to its eave side. It meets the receiving hip at a 22.5° plan angle. Its physical end follows the hip’s side plane; the rafter does not continue to the hip axis or the cupola.

The viewer’s Inspect rafter-to-hip joints button brings together R0, J0-0 and J7-2. Select either rafter, then a recorded contact surface, to see its angles, boundary lengths and vertices. The model repeats this arrangement around all eight bays.

Metal components and the cupola

The single reusable green aid retains a symbolic shape between the outward corners of neighboring cupola posts. Their 28⅛ in setting-out gap is not a flat-metal blank length. Metal thickness, end returns, holes and fastening remain unspecified.

The black fitting is one temporary welded-support display assembly: eight radial vertical-plane L straps and three holes per outer leg, 24 total. The owner-confirmed opposite-end span is 90 in (7 ft 6 in), distinct from the unchanged 88 in cupola. The reference drawing gives 4 in strap depth and 14 in total outer-leg height. Thickness, hole diameter and centers, constant-width leg interpretation and elevation are explicitly illustrative display assumptions, not cut, drilling, weld, screw or erection specifications. No erection capacity, fastening or removal criterion is assigned.

Eight flat-topped cupola roof rafters now meet above the open well. Their 2 in width follows the readable 2x framing note; their 6 in depth is provisional. The optional roof surface shows the 4:12 slope and 12 in overhang. “Boston Layout” remains the wall-window source note; window dimensions and openings are unresolved.
