Eight radial triangular frames carry two short rafters in each bay. Each short rafter lands on a neighboring hip. The eight rafters that previously ran inward from wall midpoints have been removed.

Keep the measuring points distinct

The main frame is 30 ft across outside radial post faces. With the working 8 in posts, centers are 29 ft 4 in across. Eave tips are 33 ft 4 in across. The 20 in radial overhang starts at the outside post face.

The cupola is 7 ft 4 in across outside faces and 6 ft 4 in inside. Its radial 6 × 6 in posts have a 41 in center radius. The cupola roof uses a radial 4:12 slope and a 12 in horizontal radial overhang.

A short rafter’s reference axis reaches the hip at a sloping length of 136.923 in. Its center reference reaches the hip side face at 125.811 in under the working section assumptions. Neither value includes a designed joint, stock allowance or machining clearance. All physical edges must be intersected separately to form the end.

What remains provisional

  • The 24 existing cupola-band IDs remain as contextual pieces, hidden by default. Their hidden status does not establish that they are required or that their connections are known.
  • The inventory retains 96 timber IDs: 72 visible timbers and 24 contextual bands. Metal objects and cupola roof reference surfaces are counted separately.
  • Ground-to-tie height, glazing-height endpoints, physical roof bearing, actual timber sections and roof backing still need confirmation.
  • “Boston Layout” is retained as a window note; no window openings are inferred from the blurred dimension string.
  • Old generated keys, cut-face labels, overlap reports and withdrawal tests belong to the superseded model. They do not validate this revision.

The next joint work is to fix bearing datums and compatible details at these named interfaces, then check fit, remaining section and assembly access together. The complete brief keeps each reference value and unresolved input explicit.
