Nominal joint fit checked.

The cupola posts join the lower ties at the bottom datum. Two source-supported octagonal timber bands frame the open well; eight roof rafters close above it. Main hips have flat tops, with no backing bevel.

The lower cupola ring was an unsupported reconstruction and is removed. Use P0 corner close-up, Cupola connections or Inspect rafter-to-hip joints in the viewer. The exploded-view slider stays visible. Select a timber to reveal its bright, transparent sockets and notches.

Complementary shapes

Each proposed tongue and socket uses the same cutting solid. The independent cavity-fit audit compares actual recesses with mating timber using occupancy and cross-section checks. Every permanent timber pair is also tested for positive-volume overlap, each registered connection for contact, and each timber for a single valid connected solid. The false pointed cupola-cavity overlay is corrected; invalid residual diagnostic shapes are no longer displayed.

Centered shoulder tenons, diagonal brace sockets and shallow roof bearing seats are explicit draft details. Tenon insertion of 2 in, width of one third of the member and 1 in depth shoulders are design assumptions. They are not recovered fabrication dimensions or a structural rating.

Source dimensions retained

Cupola: 7 ft 4 in outside, 6 ft 4 in inside, 41 in post-center radius, approximately 2 ft 4⅛ in outward-corner gap. Roof: radial 4:12 with 12 in radial overhang. Upper and sill bands: readable 4 × 6 callouts. Lower radial ties: 6 × 10 as previously directed by Stewart, overriding the source 6 × 8 callout.

The roof bearing offset and ring height account for timber depth. Ground height, the exact glazing-height endpoints, cupola roof timber depth, pegs and steel attachments remain unverified. No permanent member crosses the bottom opening.

Zero nominal overlap and shared contact surfaces establish mathematical fit. Load capacity, shrinkage allowances, tension restraint, machining and an executable raising sequence still require design verification.
