Geometry and connection review

R01 is based on the corrected eight-frame pattern. The mathematical checks cover the 30-foot outside-post datum, 33-foot-4-inch eave-tip span, paired short rafters, compatible roof slopes, cupola spacing and physical hip-side contact planes.

Geometry basisDimensions, angles, retained pieces and unresolved datums.Connection familiesWhat the revised geometry establishes and what remains to detail.Change registerTraceable current geometry changes and retired timber IDs.
The R01 checks establish geometric relationships. They do not establish structural capacity, finished joinery, machining clearances, full-member collision clearance or an erection sequence.

Historical analysis

The earlier carved prototype used assumed 40% keys and eight extra midpoint rafters. Its overlap, recess, net-section and insertion reports apply only to that earlier geometry.
